Finite sawtooth prefix, uniform in the slope a > 0.
For N >= 1 use the constant 2+2a on (0,a/N], and for n=N-1,...,1 use
  2+3a-(n+1)t on (a/(n+1),2a/(2n+1)],
  2+a+n t       on (2a/(2n+1),a/n].
A direct integral gives each pair's mass
 (2+2a)a(1/n-1/(n+1)) - a^2/(2n(n+1)(2n+1)).
Let H(n)=sum(k=1..n)1/k. Partial fractions, or induction, gives
 sum(n=1..N-1)1/(2n(n+1)(2n+1))
   =3/2-1/(2N)-2(H(2N)-H(N)).
Therefore the full finite-prefix mass equals
 2a+a^2/2+a^2/(2N)+2a^2(H(2N)-H(N)).
For positive x, log(x)-log(x+1) <= x/(x+1)-1=-1/(x+1).
Summing with x=N,...,2N-1 yields H(2N)-H(N)<=log 2.
Thus mass <= 2a+a^2/2+2a^2 log 2+a^2/(2N).
Choosing N sufficiently large gives every prescribed positive mass slack.
No fixed finite template is asserted to have exactly the infinite-prefix mass.
The additive per-prime C does not enter the integrated leading mass.
