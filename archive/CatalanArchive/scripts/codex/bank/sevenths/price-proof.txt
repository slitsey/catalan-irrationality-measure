A single epsilon-independent clearing price

For each actual even-scale common-deletion polynomial, crude clearing provides a
nonempty set of natural exponents e satisfying GLE(p,e,Q). Take its least element.
Nat.find_spec proves GLE for the chosen exponent, and Nat.find_min' makes it no
larger than every independently proved T/rank/LP/M3D/UF exponent on the same Q.
This removes global validity switches and keeps exactly one denominator for all
finite epsilon-templates. Exceptional-prime exponents remain present and are
bounded by Ecrude; no exceptional factor is discarded. All pointwise layer bounds
and all finite/template coverage obligations still require proof.
