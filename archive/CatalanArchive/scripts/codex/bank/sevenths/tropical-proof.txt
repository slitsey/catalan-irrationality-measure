First-level tropical entry supplier from simple pole data.
Let ell(c) be the number of singular increment indices in [1,c].
If ell(a)=ell(b), monotonicity of the singular-index sets implies there is no
singular increment in (min(a,b),max(a,b)]. Thus the harmonic-difference numerator
is p-integral. Dividing by the node difference costs one p exactly when the nodes
are congruent modulo p, since all node differences are less than p^2.
If ell(a) differs from ell(b), simple-pole data makes p times the harmonic
difference integral, and the same division costs a second p only for congruent
nodes. On the diagonal the cost is zero before the first singular index, and at
most two afterward. These are exactly the existing HW/wt weights.
The existing assignment potentials and table-T/sawtooth bounds therefore apply
to the same full matrix and all common-deletion members. At primes with p^2<=14r
use the full Ecrude price instead, paid by the higher-level small-prime budget;
fixed exceptional primes retain Ecrude in the separate fixedCrude budget.
