No-UF implementation proof notes, 28 October 2026
1. Rank JSON matrices are indexed by list positions. Simultaneous bijections from
   row/column positions to their recorded digit labels transport matrix products
   and identity matrices. The generated FibreCert uses these digit labels, and
   maps killed positions through those same bijections. Its Ok checks certify
   precisely L*Linv=I, P*Pinv=I and the killed rows/columns of L*K*P.
2. For N the lcm of every matrix entry denominator, p not dividing N implies
   p does not divide each such denominator. Thus every rational basis entry is
   in S p and the certificate is Integral p. No factoring or huge p>N bound is
   required. The finite factors of N are retained in the exceptional budget.
3. Replacing kr,kc by subsets leaves the inverse identities, pole palette and
   physical realization unchanged. Each remaining killed row/column still
   satisfies its old zero equation. This proves trim_ok and permits the
   boundary/deletion rebalancing prescribed in FIBRE-IDENTIFICATION.
4. A finite list of certified prices is combined with a certified fallback by
   repeated min. At each minimum one of the certified exponents is selected,
   so GLE holds. A new layer can only lower that pointwise price. The minimum
   over concatenated lists equals the minimum of their separate minima, since
   the fallback appears in both lists and min is idempotent. At fixed primes
   dividing M, use Ecrude exactly; their factors are never deleted.
5. The no-UF numerical contract uses the exact replay rational and log2/50,
   unchanged D,c, and Real.log_two_lt_d9 for a rational upper bound. The resulting
   ratio is <=159693/5000=31.9386; 31.9385 is unsupported by these rates.

Lambda-specific simplification of the R envelope:
The 68 JSON types have killed-row/killed-column counts (0,1), (1,0), (0,0)
or (1,1); every nonempty load has rank one and F0=0. After balancing the
number of row/column kills to K, choose V to be any recorded line. If CM and
RM count the column and row loads on V, the next gain is at least
CM-(K-RM), clipped below at zero. Thus the price is at most
2H-K-(CM+RM-K)_+ = 2H-max(K,CM+RM). The zero-space witness supplies
2H-K. A full-space witness has gain zero after balancing. This is precisely
the F+max formula in RANK-WITNESSES with F=0 and no rank-two load fibres.
rank_line_price_bound records the natural-subtraction version, including
any additional nonnegative zero-load gain F. Boundary/deletion losses in K,
CM,RM are bounded by the source strip/trim counts, hence by the specified
fixed debit and are absorbed by the additive piece constant.

Affine run lattice lower bound:
For 0<=a<=b<=p, take L=ceil(a+4), H=ceil(b-4). Every integer
in [L,H) is in [0,p), lies in the width-four trimmed run, and has
one of the two parities. Either parity occurs at least floor((H-L)/2)
times. L<a+5 and H>=b-4, while the natural difference H-L
is at least its rational signed difference. Thus either parity count
is at least (b-a)/2-5, hence also at least (b-a)/2-6.
The latter bound is kept with slack to avoid endpoint bookkeeping.

Canonical load-line witnesses:
A nonzero vector in V intersect W spans a one-dimensional subspace of
that intersection, so its finrank is at least1. A killed column load
is in Ucol by the definition of its span. A vector orthogonal to every
killed row load is in Urow by the definition as an intersection of
linear-map kernels. Thus explicit column membership and row alternating
products supply the mc/mr=1 witnesses used by RankMember, without any
claim of maximality. These facts hold for every certificate and subspace.

Global kill-count identity (canonical):
Partition the actual killed node indices by their residue modulo p.
Within one residue the digit map n->floor(n/p) is injective, because
residue and quotient determine n and the node map is injective.
Realizes and kr/kc subset R/C make its image exactly the certificate's
killed-digit set. Each fibre's actual killed count is therefore its
recorded cardinality. Summing the finite residue partition gives the
global kill count as the sum of recorded per-fibre cardinalities.

Affine count upper bound:
The trimmed run is contained in [ceil(a+4),floor(b-4)+1). On either
parity a natural interval of length d has at most(d+1)/2 points.
Its ceiling lower endpoint is at least a+4 and its upper endpoint is
at most b-3 when b>=4, so the same fixed6 slack bounds the count
above (b-a)/2. If b<4, or the interval is reversed, the run is empty.
Together with the lower bound this controls the imbalance caused by
boundary and deletion fallback, since the leading left/right kill
counts agree exactly in every recorded cell.
